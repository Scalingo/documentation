Scalingo for MySQL® is available across several service classes.
Each service class corresponds to a defined availability level and a specific
set of features.

## High Availability Setups

High availability, clustering, and SLAs are defined by the
[service class][databases-sc]. The [architecture model][architecture-models]
determines the infrastructure and availability-zone placement.

| Service class | Architecture models            | High Availability | MySQL nodes | Gateways | SLA    |
|---------------|--------------------------------|-------------------|-------------|----------|--------|
| Starter       | Shared and Dedicated Resources | No                | 1           | 1        | 98%    |
| Business      | Shared and Dedicated Resources | Yes               | 3           | 2        | 99.96% |
| Enterprise    | Dedicated Resources            | Yes               | 5           | 3        | 99.99% |

| Architecture model | Database infrastructure | Placement |
|---|---|---|
| Shared Resources | Each database node runs in a container on a shared host | Nodes and gateways remain within one availability zone |
| Dedicated Resources | Each database node runs on its own dedicated virtual machine | For HA plans, nodes and gateways are distributed across up to three availability zones |

## Single-node plans

Starter plans are running on a single node, without any redundancy or high
availability mechanism. Consequently, we don't consider them to be
*highly available*. This translates into a lower
[Service Level Agreement][databases-sc].

## Highly Available plans

Business and Enterprise plans include a fully-managed InnoDB cluster.
MySQL® Group Replication runs in single-primary mode: one primary receives
writes, while the other nodes maintain replicas of the data.
MySQL® Router instances route connections to the primary.

Communications between all instances of the cluster are encrypted.

### Business Plans

Business plans include:

- Three (3) MySQL® nodes: one primary and two secondaries.
- Two (2) MySQL® Router instances: one leader and one failover.
