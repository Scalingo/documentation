Each [Scalingo account][account] must have a **billing profile** to use the
Scalingo platform and create resources.

This profile stores all the information we need to be able to generate invoices
for the services you consume. It also allows you to choose and configure at
least one payment method.


## Filling Your Billing Profile

{% include billing_context.md %}

1. Open the [Dashboard][dashboard]
2. Open **Billing**, then select the **Billing profile** tab
3. Click **Add billing informations** to open the form
4. Select **Individual** or **Business**, depending on who should be billed, and
   complete the form
5. Click **Save these billing informations**
6. [Add a payment method][payment-add]

{% note %}
Review the billing profile type and information regularly, and update them when
they change. Accurate details help Scalingo issue invoices to the correct
individual or business, with the correct name and address.
{% endnote %}

## E-invoicing for Businesses in France

France's e-invoicing reform (facturation électronique) applies to businesses
subject to VAT and established in metropolitan France, Guadeloupe,
Martinique or Réunion.

If your business is established in one of these locations, select **Business**
and review or complete:

- Your legal business name
- Your complete, up-to-date billing address
- Your 9-digit SIREN number
- Your VAT number, if you have one

Scalingo uses these details to identify your business and route its invoices to
its approved e-invoicing platform (*plateforme agréée*, or PA). If you have
already chosen a PA, check that your legal business name, SIREN number and
address match the details registered with it.
