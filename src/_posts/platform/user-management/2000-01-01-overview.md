Each person uses a Scalingo account to sign in. With this account, you can manage
your own projects and resources, join organizations, and collaborate on resources
owned by others.

## Scalingo Account

Your Scalingo account identifies you on the platform. It holds your profile,
authentication settings, SSH keys and API tokens.

Your **personal account** is also where you manage your own projects, resources
and billing. You can invite collaborators to your resources without creating an
organization.

- [Create a Scalingo account][creating-account]
- [Navigate the Scalingo Account Dashboard][account-dashboard]

## Organizations

An organization groups projects, resources and members under its own
administration and billing. You use your Scalingo account to create or join an
organization.

The organization's **Admin** manages its members and billing and has full access
to its resources. Other members need a collaborator role on each resource they
use.

{% note %}
Organizations are currently available in preview. [Contact our Sales team][sales]
if you're interested.
{% endnote %}

- [Learn about organizations][organizations]
- [Create an organization][creating-organization]
- [Join an organization][joining-organization]

## Collaborators

A collaborator has access to a specific application or database. Their resource
role defines what they can do.

Collaborator access applies to resources in personal accounts and organizations.
In an organization, a person must first join as a member before being added as a
collaborator.

- [Manage resource collaborators][collaborators]
- [Understand resource roles][roles]
