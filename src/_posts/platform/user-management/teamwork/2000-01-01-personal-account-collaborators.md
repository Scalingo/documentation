In your personal account, you add collaborators to individual applications. The
**Collaborators** page brings these assignments together, giving you a complete
view of who has access to your applications.

## Listing Collaborators

### Using the Dashboard

1. From your web browser, open your [dashboard][dashboard]
2. Select **Personal account**
3. The consolidated list of all users collaborating on all your apps, along
   with their 2FA status, is available from the **Collaborators** tab
4. (optional) The **Group by** buttons allow to group the list either by
   collaborator or by app.


## Exporting Collaborators

### Using the Dashboard

1. From your web browser, open your [dashboard][dashboard]
2. Select **Personal account**
3. Click the **Collaborators** tab
4. Click the **Export as CSV** button to export the list of collaborators in
   CSV format. The list is also available in JSON format from the drop-down.

[dashboard]: https://dashboard.scalingo.com/
