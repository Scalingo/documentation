Scalingo allows you to easily collaborate with others while maintaining full
control over your applications, databases and environments.

To work together on an application or database, add collaborators and assign each
one a role that defines what they can do.

Follow the instructions to manage collaborators on:

- [Applications and their add-ons][managing-collaborators]
- [PostgreSQL Dedicated Resources databases][postgresql-collaborators]
- [MySQL Dedicated Resources databases][mysql-collaborators]

You can add as many collaborators as needed.

We currently provide three predefined resource roles to help you adapt permissions to
the level of responsibility you want to delegate to your collaborators:
[**Owner**][role-owner], [**Collaborator**][role-collaborator] and
[**Limited Collaborator**][role-limited-collaborator].

See our [best practices][best-practices] for recommendations on managing user access.
