An organization groups projects, resources and members under its own
administration and billing. Each member can access only the resources they
have permission to use. Resource usage is billed to the organization.

{% note %}
Organizations are currently available in preview. [Contact our Sales team][sales]
if you're interested.
{% endnote %}


## When to Use Organizations

Use an organization when a group of projects needs its own administration,
members and billing.

For example:

- **Client projects:** Create an organization for a client and give them the
  **Admin** role so they can manage billing, while your agency works on the
  resources as members and collaborators.
- **Company projects:** Group your company's projects in an organization where
  employees collaborate and resource usage is billed to the company.
- **Independent teams or business units:** Create separate organizations when
  departments or subsidiaries need different administrators, members or billing
  arrangements.

To occasionally collaborate on an application, you can continue inviting
collaborators to applications in your personal account without creating an
organization.


[Create an organization][creating] to get started.


## Accessing an Organization in the Dashboard

1. Open your [Dashboard][dashboard].
2. Open the **My organizations** menu in the navigation bar.
3. Select the organization you want to work in.

The Dashboard opens the organization's home page. The organization's name
remains visible in the navigation bar, so you always know which context you are
working in.

{% note %}
Select **Personal account** to return to your own resources.
{% endnote %}

You can also open [**My organizations**][my-organizations] from your user
profile to list the organizations you are a member of, then click an
organization's name to open it.


## Managing an Organization

The member with the **Admin** role manages the organization's members, projects,
resources and billing. Other members see only the projects containing resources
they can access.

### Organization Roles

**Admin**
: Invites and removes members, creates projects and resources, manages resource
  access and billing, and monitors consumption.

**Member**
: Collaborates on the resources they have access to, according to their
  [resource role][resource-roles].

### Members and Resource Access

Membership and resource access are separate: joining an organization does not
automatically grant access to its resources. Members need a collaborator role on
each resource they use.

Learn how to [manage organization members][members] and
[manage resource collaborators][collaborators].

### Billing

The organization has its own billing information and payment methods.

Learn how to [complete the billing profile][billing-profile],
[add a payment method][payment-methods] and [monitor consumption][consumption].


## Current Limits

- Each user can create up to 10 organizations.
- Each organization has a single administrator.
- Only members with the **Admin** role can create projects and resources.
- Organizations are not eligible for the [free trial][free-trial].
- You cannot delete an organization that still has consumption. Contact
  [our support team][support] to close it.
- If you are an organization's **Admin**, you cannot
  [delete your Scalingo account][delete-account] while the organization is still
  attached to it. Contact [our support team][support] first.
