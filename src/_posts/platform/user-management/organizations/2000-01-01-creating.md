## Creating an Organization

1. Open your [Dashboard][dashboard].
2. Click **Create a new organization**.
3. Enter an **Organization name** between 3 and 64 characters.
4. Click **Create organization**.

The Dashboard opens the new organization's context. You become its sole
administrator.

## Next Steps

1. [Complete the billing profile][billing-profile] and
   [add a valid payment method][payment-methods] before creating resources.
2. [Create a project][creating-projects], then [create an application][creating-apps],
   or [transfer an existing personal project to the organization][transfer-project].
3. [Invite users to join the organization][members] and
   [give them access to resources][collaborators].
