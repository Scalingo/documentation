Each person needs a Scalingo account to sign in, including when working in an
organization.

Your personal account is where you manage your own projects, resources and billing.
You can also use the same Scalingo account to create or join [organizations], which
have their own projects, resources, members and billing.

There are two ways to create your Scalingo account:
- [Using an e-mail address and password](#using-an-e-mail-address)
- [Using GitHub Single Sign On](#using-github-sso) (SSO)


## Using an E-Mail Address

1. Open the [sign up][sign-up] page
2. Either select **Business** or **Personal**, depending on your situation
3. Enter the e-mail address you want to use for your Scalingo account
4. Choose a **strong** password
5. Validate by clicking the **Sign up with email** button


## Using GitHub SSO

1. Open the [sign up][sign-up] page
2. Click the **Sign up with GitHub** button
3. You are redirected to GitHub where you are asked to grant Scalingo access to
   specific personal information associated with your GitHub account. This
   allows us to check your identity and get your e-mail address (the one
   associated with your GitHub account)

{% note %}
Signing up with GitHub gives you access to additional [SCM integration features][scm].
{% endnote %}


## After Creating Your Account

- **Create your own resources:** Start your [free trial][free-trial] to explore
  the platform.
- **Accept an invitation:** Join an organization or collaborate on a resource.
