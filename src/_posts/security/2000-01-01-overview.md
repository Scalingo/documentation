## English {#en}

Scalingo's security documentation describes controls for protecting applications and data, securing platform operations,
responding to security events, and planning for service continuity. The pages below explain each area. For published
compliance and certification information, see [Compliance][compliance]; [Auditing][auditing] provides an overview of
controls and audits.

### Platform and data protection

[Application security][application-security], [buildpack security][buildpack-security], [network security][network-security],
[connectivity][connectivity], [cryptography][cryptography], [data protection][data-protection], and [backups][backups].

### Secure development and operations

[Code security][code-security], [operational security][operational-security], and [monitoring and log management][monitoring].

### People, suppliers, and facilities

[Employee security][employee-security], [third-party security][third-parties], and [physical security][physical-security].

### Incident response and continuity

[Incidents and support][incident-support], [business continuity and disaster recovery][business-continuity],
and [post-mortems][post-mortems].

### Assurance and security notices

Read about [compliance][compliance] and [auditing][auditing], review the published [security procedures][security-procedures],
and follow [security bulletins][security-bulletins].

## Français {#fr}

La documentation de sécurité de Scalingo présente les mesures de protection des applications et des données, de sécurisation
de l'exploitation de la plateforme, de réponse aux événements de sécurité et de préparation à la continuité de service.
Les pages ci-dessous détaillent chaque sujet. Pour les informations publiées sur la conformité et les certifications,
consultez la page [Conformité][compliance] ; la page [Audits][auditing] présente un aperçu des contrôles et des audits.

### Personnel, fournisseurs et locaux

### Gestion des incidents et continuité
