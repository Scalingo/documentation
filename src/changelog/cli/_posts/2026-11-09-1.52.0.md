### Installation

[https://cli.scalingo.com](https://cli.scalingo.com)

### Changelog

* Add commands to list, add, and remove application firewall rules: `app-firewall-rules`, `app-firewall-rule-add`, and `app-firewall-rule-remove`.
* Add the `organizations` command (alias `orgs`) to list your organizations and your role in each organization.
* Update go-scalingo to v11.9.0 to support listing organizations.
