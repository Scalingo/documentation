PHP `8.6.0` is now available!

Due to breaking changes, the following extensions are **not available yet**
with PHP `8.6.0`:
- [`amqp`](https://github.com/php-amqp/php-amqp)
- [`apcu`](https://github.com/krakjoe/apcu)
- [`igbinary`](https://github.com/igbinary/igbinary)
- [`imagick`](https://github.com/Imagick/imagick)
- [`memcached`](https://github.com/php-memcached-dev/php-memcached)
- [`mongodb`](https://github.com/mongodb/mongo-php-driver)
- [`redis`](https://github.com/phpredis/phpredis/)


Changelog:
- [PHP 8.6.0](https://www.php.net/ChangeLog-8.php#8.6.0)
