Python `3.12.16`, `3.13.18`, `3.14.10` and `3.15.2` are now available.

These are also the new default version for, respectively, Python 3.12, 3.13,
3.14, and 3.15.

Python `3.11` is now deprecated and no longer supported on Scalingo.

Changelogs:
- [`3.15.2`](https://docs.python.org/3.15/whatsnew/changelog.html#python-3.15.2-final)
- [`3.14.10`](https://docs.python.org/3.14/whatsnew/changelog.html#python-3.14.10-final)
- [`3.13.18`](https://docs.python.org/3.13/whatsnew/changelog.html#python-3.13.18-final)
- [`3.12.16`](https://docs.python.org/3.12/whatsnew/changelog.html#python-3.12.16-final)
