Python `3.11.17`, `3.12.15`, `3.13.16` and `3.14.8` are now available.

These are also the new default version for, respectively, Python 3.11, 3.12,
3.13 and 3.14.

- [Python 3.12.15](https://docs.python.org/3.12/whatsnew/changelog.html#python-3.12.15-final)
- [Python 3.11.17](https://docs.python.org/3.11/whatsnew/changelog.html#python-3.11.17-final)
