1. From your web browser, open your [dashboard](https://dashboard.scalingo.com/apps)
2. Click on the application containing the database you want to manage
3. Click on the **Resources** tab
4. Locate the **Addons** block and open the **…** menu next to the database you
   want to manage
5. Select **Change plan**
6. Select the new plan
7. Click the **Finish** button
8. Confirm by clicking the **Confirm plan change** button
